## TYPOGRAPHY

Functional grotesk for reading.
Monospace for system labels, timestamps and metadata.

Suggested:
Geist / Inter
IBM Plex Mono

## GRID

12 columns on desktop.
Large negative space.
Editorial asymmetry.
Strong lines and dividers.
Visible system numbering.

## CONTROLS

Physical references:
- knob;
- slider;
- push button;
- toggle;
- LED;
- segment display.

Use skeuomorphism sparingly and functionally.

## CENTRAL PRESENCE

Abstract orb.
No human avatar.

States:
IDLE
LISTENING
THINKING
SPEAKING
ACTING
ERROR

Motion is restrained and physical.

Dividers.
Editorial modules.
Intentional asymmetry.
